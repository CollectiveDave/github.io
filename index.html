Introduction

Gaming has been part of my life for over four decades, across just about every genre and platform.

My relationship with games goes well beyond playing them. I started working on games from home in 1994, contributing to Usurper, a DOS-based BBS game. That early experience eventually grew into a career spanning decades in game development, production, QA, and product leadership.

I'm very familiar with the gaming industry, its communities, competitive environments, and the design decisions that keep players engaged. My experience spans a broad range of games and genres, giving me an understanding of game mechanics, player behavior, community culture, and the factors that contribute to a game's long-term success. I appreciate how thoughtful design, strong communities, and engaging experiences help games stand out and build lasting player relationships.
