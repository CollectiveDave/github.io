Introduction

Gaming has been part of my life for over four decades, across just about every genre and platform.

My relationship with games goes well beyond playing them. I started working on games from home in 1994, contributing to Usurper, a DOS-based BBS game. That early experience eventually grew into a career spanning decades in game development, production, QA, and product leadership.

I'm very familiar with the gaming industry, its communities, competitive environments, and the design decisions that keep players engaged. My familiarity with League of Legends comes from understanding the MOBA genre, its mechanics, competitive ecosystem, and player culture. While my professional experience spans a broad range of games, I appreciate what makes League a lasting and influential part of the industry.
